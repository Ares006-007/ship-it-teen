# ShipIt Teens

**Build. Learn. Ship. Together.**

ShipIt Teens is a community of teen builders who learn by making things that matter. We bring together curious students who are ready to move beyond tutorials and into real projects, shared experiments, and meaningful collaboration.

Our community welcomes people interested in software, hardware, open source, and ambitious side projects. You do not need permission or a perfect plan to begin. Bring your curiosity, your ideas, and your willingness to build in public.

---

## What we do

We create space for young builders to learn through doing.

- **Open projects:** Build software, contribute to open source, and share your work publicly.
- **Hardware experiments:** Explore circuits, Raspberry Pis, robotics, and hands-on invention.
- **Demo days:** Showcase what you make, get feedback, and celebrate shipping.
- **Community experiences:** Join builder-focused events and experiences shared across the site.

---

## The ShipIt mindset

We believe learning happens by building, testing, and iterating.

- Build in public.
- Ship early and learn by doing.
- Choose momentum over perfection.
- Stay curious, collaborate often, and create work that matters.

ShipIt is not about collecting credentials or waiting until you feel like an expert. It is about learning alongside other teens who are excited to create, share, and improve.

---

## Why join

Whether you are just getting started or already building ambitious projects, ShipIt Teens is a place to:

- learn from others who are building real things
- get feedback on ideas and prototypes
- find collaborators for projects and experiments
- grow confidence by shipping work, not just consuming tutorials
